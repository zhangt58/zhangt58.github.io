## Today's Top 10 on Hacker News

### 1. [Local Wikipedia: Wikipedia, but only for things a half hour walk from you](https://edjefferson.com/localwiki/)
⭐ 462 💬 111 👤 sohkamyung
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50202285)

Local Wikipedia is a website that presents Wikipedia content limited to places within about a half-hour walk of the user's location. It uses geocoded Wikipedia pages and, depending on density, shows the closest 25 to 500 nearby entries. The user's location remains in the browser and is not included in shared links, and the site is not affiliated with Wikipedia.

### 2. [Docker is weird](https://codeandcake.dev/posts/2026-10-22-docker-is-weird)
⭐ 525 💬 233 👤 psxuaw
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50202681)

Docker containers are not virtual machines but ordinary Linux processes isolated by a combination of kernel features such as namespaces, cgroups, and networking, while images are simply filesystem folders used as a chroot. The runc runtime is written in Go, which complicates the single-threaded syscalls and fork/exec behavior needed for containers, and Docker traditionally requires root access, making rootless operation especially difficult for networking. Building a custom runtime reveals how much of Docker’s behavior depends on low-level Linux mechanisms.

### 5. [The foolishness of "natural language programming" (1978)](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/transcriptions/EWD06xx/EWD667.html)
⭐ 192 💬 113 👤 xiaoyu2006
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50203161)

The article argues that natural language programming is misguided because programming requires the precision of formal symbolism, and machines must follow instructions exactly. It contends that shifting more burden to the machine through natural-language interfaces does not simplify human work and may increase complexity. The author points to the history of mathematics, where formal notation enabled progress, to show that formal languages are a powerful tool for avoiding ambiguity and nonsense.

### 6. [The alien space of science: Coherent but cognitively unavailable directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01092)
⭐ 77 💬 18 👤 martincsweiss
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50195002)

The article proposes a framework for identifying scientifically plausible research directions that are unlikely to be pursued by existing communities because no group combines the needed concepts and methods. It decomposes papers into conceptual units, scores combinations for scientific coherence and community availability, and ranks ideas that are coherent but cognitively unavailable. Experiments on machine learning papers show the approach generates more diverse and competitive research portfolios than language-model baselines and can recover future work better than chance.

### 7. [Every AI model has the same "controversial" take on humans](https://www.modelcompare.ai/blog/ai-models-controversial-take-on-humans)
⭐ 444 💬 445 👤 rakannimer
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50197313)

Researchers asked 12 AI models from multiple labs to state their most controversial opinion about humans, with no hedging, three times each. The dominant response was that people prioritize belonging, tribal loyalty, and social approval over truth, often inventing rational justifications after making instinctive or group-driven decisions. The result suggests that many AI models share a similar skeptical view of human rationality and moral conviction.

### 8. [The HTML-in-Canvas API origin trial](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/html-in-canvas-origin-trial)
⭐ 54 💬 25 👤 andrewstuart
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50200164)

The HTML-in-Canvas API lets developers render DOM content directly into a 2D canvas or WebGL/WebGPU texture while preserving interactivity, accessibility, and browser features. It removes the traditional trade-off between semantic HTML interfaces and low-level canvas graphics, enabling richer web experiences such as selectable text, form controls, find-in-page, and extension support inside canvas-based scenes.

### 9. [OpenShell – A safe, private runtime for fleets of autonomous AI agents](https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenShell)
⭐ 408 💬 195 👤 niemandhier
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198675)

OpenShell is a runtime that lets fleets of autonomous AI agents access files, packages, APIs, and credentials while enforcing declared policies to protect data, secrets, and networks. It enforces rules at the kernel level for every file access, system call, and network connection, and uses formal verification to validate policy changes before applying them. Version 0.1.x adds a stable release cadence, new isolation primitives, an expanded extension surface, and new APIs.
