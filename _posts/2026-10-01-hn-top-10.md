### 6. [Why the Bronze Age Collapsed](https://www.worksinprogress.news/p/why-really-caused-the-bronze-age)
⭐ 255 💬 161 👤 AnodicElegy
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49890732)

The article examines the Bronze Age collapse, when major eastern Mediterranean empires such as Egypt, the Hittites, Assyria, Babylonia, and Mycenaean Greece suddenly declined. It argues that familiar causes like drought, famine, and the Sea Peoples do not explain why complex empires failed to re-emerge for centuries. Instead, the spread of iron weapons, which could be produced locally, weakened the military advantage of imperial elites and permanently altered the political balance.

### 10. [Adding Floating-Point Decimals for Fun and Profit](https://blog.vero.site/post/float)
⭐ 6 💬 0 👤 ibobev
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49893519)

The article explains why decimal calculations in programming languages can produce small rounding errors because decimal values like 0.1 cannot be represented exactly in binary floating-point format.
