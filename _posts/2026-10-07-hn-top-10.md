### 1. [A font recreated from photographs of classic Commodore 64 keycaps](https://github.com/szabadkai/c64-keyboard-font/)
⭐ 63 💬 9 👤 sohkamyung
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49990224)

### 9. [ESP32-C3 Adblock](https://github.com/M-Abozaid/esp32-c3-adblock)
⭐ 107 💬 41 👤 jayhoon
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49986862)

A Pi-hole-style DNS ad-blocker that runs on a $2 ESP32-C3 with no PSRAM. Instead of keeping the blocklist in RAM, it stores 140,000+ domains as sorted 40-bit FNV-1a hashes in flash (~0.7 MB) and binary-searches them, using only ~50 KB of RAM with ~10 ms lookups. Ad domains are sinkholed to 0.0.0.0 while everything else is forwarded to an upstream resolver. The trick — hash-in-flash instead of string-in-RAM — cuts the hardware cost from ~$8 to ~$2.

### 10. [What is Codemode](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/10/6/codemode/)
⭐ 92 💬 43 👤 Tomte
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49978333)

Codemode is a Pi 1.0 feature that adds MCP support by letting an LLM orchestrate tool calls from a sandboxed harness-side runtime instead of loading many custom tools into its context. It separates the trusted harness, which plans and composes operations, from the execution environment where tools actually run. This lets models express complex workflows in code while keeping the execution target isolated and secure.
