## Today's Top 10 on Hacker News

### 1. [Eight Bits of Trust; eMudhra's CA keys cracked in milliseconds](https://scotthelme.co.uk/eight-bits-of-trust-emudhras-ca-keys-cracked-in-milliseconds/)
⭐ 138 💬 54 👤 hkc
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50177916)

A security researcher found that eight eMudhra certificate authority private keys were extremely weak single-byte values, allowing anyone to recover them from public certificates in milliseconds. The flaw affected six TLS issuing CAs and two code-signing CAs, prompting browsers to block the affected intermediates and remove or plan to remove the eMudhra root. Because the keys were inherently weak rather than stolen, the compromise could not be contained by revoking certificates alone.

### 5. [Whatever Happened to Fire Escapes](https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/10/20/whatever-happened-to-fire-escapes)
⭐ 12 💬 1 👤 sohkamyung
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50178455)

Fire escapes emerged in 19th-century American cities as a response to deadly fires in tall tenements and factories, offering an external evacuation route when interior stairwells were blocked. Their adoption and later decline reflect how building codes balance safety, cost, and practicality, since owners rarely install safety features voluntarily and codes set minimum standards. Although still visible on older buildings, fire escapes have gradually disappeared as modern codes and safety systems evolved.

### 8. [The Decoherence Trap](https://maevyn.codes/decoherence)
⭐ 54 💬 14 👤 cassianoleal
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50149861)

The article defines decoherence as the buildup of many locally useful but mutually incompatible or redundant models, making change expensive because systems must constantly translate among them and avoid side effects. ### 9. [eMudhra intermediate CA compromise](https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/)
⭐ 988 💬 414 👤 galadran
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50166579)

The private keys for eight eMudhra intermediate CA certificates have been compromised, allowing an attacker to potentially impersonate any website or service. The affected certificates chain to roots trusted by major browser and operating-system trust stores. Mozilla, Microsoft, Apple, and Chrome have blocked the certificates, and eMudhra has revoked them, though some clients may still need updates to stop trusting them.

⭐ 16 💬 3 👤 frizlab
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50150214)

A fan-made Apple Vision Pro project turns Super Mario 64 into a table-top diorama, letting players walk around each level and view it from any angle while using a game controller. It also highlights a broader tension, as Nintendo has previously opposed similar PC ports while other companies have embraced rebuilt classic games.
