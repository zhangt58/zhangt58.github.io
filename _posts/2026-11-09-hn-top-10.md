### 10. [Outlook.com is rejecting email from domains without DMARC](https://mailroute.net/blog/outlook-rejecting-email-without-dmarc)
⭐ 426 💬 302 👤 xmddmx
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50416158)

Microsoft's Outlook.com, Hotmail, Live, and MSN mail services have begun rejecting some messages from domains that lack a DMARC record, even for low-volume senders. The issue can cause intermittent bounces without clear notice, making it easy to miss. Adding a single DMARC DNS TXT record, starting with v=DMARC1; p=none, can resolve the problem if SPF or DKIM is aligned with the From address.
