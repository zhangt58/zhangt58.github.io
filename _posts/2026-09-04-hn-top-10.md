### 2. [.name Termination](https://neil.fraser.name/news/2026/09/03/)
⭐ 1872 💬 459 👤 pavel_lishin
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550772)

In a personal account, Neil Fraser describes how Verisign proposed destroying the entire third level of the .name domain hierarchy and ICANN approved it in July. The change will terminate 22,000 registered names — including his, paid for until 2040 — wiping out his website, email address, and IoT services in February. He warns that the vacant second-level domains could then be re-registered by strangers, letting them hijack decades of accounts tied to those addresses.

### 8. [Hackers Had a Live Feed of Every ID Verification Company Scanned for over a Year](https://www.techdirt.com/2026/09/03/hackers-had-a-live-feed-of-every-id-this-verification-company-scanned-for-over-a-year/)

### 10. [Artificial beaver dams saw juvenile coho salmon survival rates go from 8% to 60%](https://www.discoverwildlife.com/animal-facts/artificial-beaver-dams-california)
⭐ 269 💬 85 👤 speckx
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49552572)

In California's Scott River valley, where 1830s fur trappers wiped out the beavers whose dams created coho salmon habitat, the Scott River Watershed Council built artificial beaver dams from posts, willow, conifer, and mud. The structures recreated about 9,000 square metres of cold wetland habitat, and juvenile coho survival on French Creek jumped from 8% before the dams to 60% after.
