### 5. [Built Dental Scope](https://dental-scope.com/)
⭐ 29 💬 6 👤 Zeruxe
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49886535)

Dental Scope is an open-source, interactive 3D explorer that lets users examine dental anatomy from the whole mouth down to individual tooth structures such as pulp and root canals.
