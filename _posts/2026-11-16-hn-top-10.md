### 1. [Coffeehouses and the Rise of Science [pdf]](https://www.monirbounadi.com/Bounadi_JMP.pdf)
⭐ 33 💬 12 👤 Michelangelo11
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50516245)

A study of London and European cities finds that the opening of the first coffeehouse in a locality was followed by lasting increases in applied scientists and scientific instrument shops. The effects were strongest among scientists with weak ties to Oxford and Cambridge, suggesting coffeehouses helped connect practical craft knowledge with theoretical science. A quantitative model estimates that coffeehouses accounted for about 31 percent of London's applied scientists in the following half century, indicating that such social institutions expanded access to intellectual exchange beyond formal academic networks.

### 5. [Researchers track whole body movement with just a smartwatch](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/11/researchers-track-whole-body-movement-just-smartwatch)
⭐ 74 💬 14 👤 PaulHoule
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50486745)

Researchers have developed EchoMotion, a system that tracks a person's full-body movement using only the speaker and microphone in an off-the-shelf smartwatch. It emits ultrasonic sound waves and uses a lightweight AI model to interpret echoes from the wearer's body, offering a private, low-power alternative to cameras and complex sensor setups. The technology could support fitness tracking, daily activity monitoring, virtual reality, and physical rehabilitation.

### 8. [Exact Gradients Through a BRep Kernel](https://muni.works/research/vcad)
⭐ 15 💬 1 👤 ecto
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50487628)

This article describes a method for computing exact parameter derivatives in a boundary-representation CAD kernel by freezing the tessellation topology and differentiating node positions using dual numbers and implicit differentiation. It extends the approach to analytic fillet sensitivities, directional second derivatives, and a reverse-mode pass, enabling gradient-based optimization of mass properties and rigid-body contact. The method is validated against finite differences, with noted limitations including polygonization error and failures when topology changes.
