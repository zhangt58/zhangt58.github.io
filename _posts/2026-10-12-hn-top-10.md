### 3. [Fingerprints are the new cookies](https://royfish.dev/fingerprints-are-the-new-cookies)
⭐ 406 💬 208 👤 royfish
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50048642)

Device fingerprinting is becoming a replacement for third-party cookies, allowing websites and ad networks to identify users across sessions and devices by combining browser, operating system, IP, and other technical signals. The article argues this tracking enables detailed profiling and data trading, while major tech companies have shifted from promising cookie deprecation to embracing fingerprinting. ### 4. [Postgres isn't slow, your storage is](https://clickhouse.com/blog/posette-talk-recap-postgres-isnt-slow-your-storage-is)
⭐ 105 💬 77 👤 saisrirampur
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50052163)

The article argues that many PostgreSQL performance problems at scale, such as slow ingestion, erratic read latency, vacuum lag, checkpoint spikes, and replication delay, are often caused by storage limitations rather than the database itself. A benchmark comparing the same large workload on network-attached EBS and local NVMe shows that local NVMe sharply reduces I/O cost, improving throughput and tail latency. It then outlines production patterns for running PostgreSQL on local NVMe to avoid these bottlenecks.

[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50045984)

### 6. [Running the unmodified Linux amdgpu driver on an iPad](https://github.com/Geramy/LemonSeed-Studio)
⭐ 33 💬 13 👤 joshcsimmons
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50041573)

LemonSeed Studio is an iPad app that runs the unmodified Linux amdgpu and amdkfd drivers through a PCIDriverKit extension, allowing an external AMD GPU in a Thunderbolt enclosure to be used on iPad. It includes an inference engine that executes on-device LLM workloads via the GPU's HSA runtime, along with live GPU telemetry. The project is available under either the MIT or GPL-2.0-only license.

### 9. [Show HN: PS2 games statically recompiled to WebAssembly](https://github.com/snuri00/ps2-web-recomp)
⭐ 42 💬 12 👤 deadxwarrior
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50052251)

A project statically recompiles PlayStation 2 games into WebAssembly so they run directly in a browser without an emulator. The process converts the Emotion Engine code into C++, then compiles it to WebAssembly and links it with a reimplementation of PS2 system software and hardware that renders using WebGPU. Demonstrations include Gran Turismo 4 and God of War II, which run at full speed from user-supplied disc images.
