### 4. [Rust 1.100](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/11/12/Rust-1.100.0/)
⭐ 721 💬 264 👤 mbStavola
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50472038)

Rust 1.100.0 is a new stable release that stabilizes the never type, allowing diverging computations to be named as ! and coerced to any type. It also introduces new APIs such as Allocator and DropGuard, along with Cargo improvements including unused dependency reporting, reduced cache collisions, and a minimum publish age feature. The release includes additional stabilized standard library APIs and some edition-related changes to never type fallback.

### 10. [Dwarf planet candidate appears which takes roughly 280 years to orbit our sun](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/astronomers-found-a-mysterious-object-orbiting-far-beyond-pluto-it-could-be-a-new-dwarf-planet-hidden-in-our-solar-system-180989857/)
⭐ 327 💬 135 👤 cybermango
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50472490)

Astronomers have identified a new dwarf planet candidate, 2026 ED138, that orbits the Sun about 46 times farther than Earth and takes roughly 280 years to complete one orbit. The object was found while analyzing data from the Vera C. Rubin Observatory and confirmed using historical images, including plates from 1937. Its final classification depends on determining whether it is large enough to meet the criteria for a dwarf planet.
