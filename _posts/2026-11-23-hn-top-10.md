### 9. [Perplexity and Intelligence](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/)
⭐ 605 💬 405 👤 acossta
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50601525)

The article argues that perplexity, or cross-entropy loss, is a central metric for tracking AI progress because it measures how well a model predicts the next token during pretraining. It suggests that publishing such measurements can help the industry establish shared safety standards and compare models more transparently. The post also traces how decreasing perplexity has coincided with major capability jumps, from coherent text generation to reasoning and advanced problem-solving.

### 10. [A Partial Evaluator for C](https://phoenix.labri.fr/software/tempo/)
⭐ 71 💬 20 👤 measurablefunc
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50607627)

Tempo is a partial evaluator for C that automates program specialization by analyzing which inputs are known and unknown, then generating a specialized program. It performs binding-time, alias, and side-effect analyses to track values across procedures, pointers, and data structures, supporting both compile-time source-to-source transformation and run-time binary code generation. It has been applied in areas such as operating systems, networking, graphics, scientific computation, and domain-specific languages.
