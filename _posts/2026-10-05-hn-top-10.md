⭐ 817 💬 361 👤 snehesht
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49953495)

Strata is a free, open-source tool that runs the 125B-parameter Qwen3.8-Flash-Next model on a normal gaming PC with 12 GB or more of VRAM (NVIDIA or AMD), on Windows or Linux. On an RTX 5070 it writes at about 94 tokens/s (Q2_0 quantization) and ingests a 32K-token prompt at roughly 2,650 tokens/s, with a 24 GB RTX 3090 expected to hit 100–140 tokens/s. ### 10. [Gods in the Classroom: Religion and Education in First Millennium BCE Babylonia](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/iraq/article/gods-in-the-classroom-religion-and-education-in-1st-millennium-bce-babylonia/EB059EE81804DCBC1848CACBD6298E0B)
⭐ 41 💬 18 👤 pseudolus
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49920332)

In first-millennium BCE Babylonia, religious education was not a separate subject but a set of specialized knowledge and practices transmitted to elites, including priests, scribes, officials, and other specialists. Religious texts were woven into broader school curricula according to the skills students needed, alongside social and cultural norms. As a result, education in Mesopotamia did not distinguish between secular and religious learning, even when schools were located in temple complexes.
