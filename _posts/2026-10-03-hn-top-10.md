### 1. [ADHD, autism or complex trauma? [pdf]](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/30CC4826561366615BFAEC807CDE28A7/S0007125026108046a.pdf/adhd-autism-or-complex-trauma-the-complicated-nature-of-the-question.pdf)

The article examines the growing difficulty of distinguishing ADHD, autism, and complex trauma in adults, as rising self-diagnosis and social media amplify demand for assessment. ### 4. [FTL: A new operating system for clouds](https://ftl-os.org/)
⭐ 94 💬 43 👤 romac
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49944912)

FTL is a cloud-oriented operating system that runs each container as a userspace OS implemented as a shared library, allowing developers to add and update OS features like application code. Its lightweight kernel provides hypervisor-like isolation for containers while remaining compatible with Linux binaries and supporting specialized unikernel-style workloads. The design aims to make containers as secure as virtual machines without sacrificing performance.

### 9. [Body Awareness in Goffin's Cockatoos](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-57500-7)
⭐ 25 💬 3 👤 bryanrasmussen
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49913106)

The article examines body awareness in Goffin's cockatoos by questioning the mirror self-recognition test as the main measure of self-awareness, since it relies heavily on vision and may not suit all species. It introduces the body-as-an-obstacle task, which tests whether animals recognize their own body as an obstacle to reaching a goal, offering a more ecologically valid and species-appropriate way to study self-representation. This approach helps clarify how nonhuman animals, including birds, may understand their bodies in relation to their environment.
