### 3. [Loom seamlessly combines weaving, knitting and braiding](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/11/loom-seamlessly-combines-weaving-knitting-and-braiding)
⭐ 152 💬 17 👤 PaulHoule

Cornell researchers have created Kneadix, a prototype loom that blends weaving, braiding, and knitting-like processes into a single machine. The team hopes the technology will support advanced garments, smart clothing, and eventually affordable maker kits.

### 8. [Paolo Soleri built a utopian eco-city in the Arizona desert](https://www.worldofinteriors.com/story/paolo-soleri-arconsati-arizona)
⭐ 84 💬 20 👤 robtherobber
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50666724)

Arcosanti is Paolo Soleri’s unfinished 860-acre prototype city in the Arizona desert, conceived as a compact, three-dimensional alternative to suburban sprawl. Based on his idea of arcology, the Brutalist concrete community was planned for thousands but has remained a small experimental collective where residents share simple, communal living. Its focus on density, sustainability, and shared space makes it feel increasingly relevant amid modern housing and urban challenges.

### 9. [Credit card skimming scripts using WebRTC found on popular shopping sites](https://www.infohawk.com/news/2026-11-27-black-friday-webrtc-skimmer)
⭐ 542 💬 281 👤 scanr
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50668145)

Card-skimming scripts were discovered on the online stores of Lascana, Samsonite, American Tourister, Revolve, and FWRD, where they used WebRTC to steal payment and personal details before a store could validate a purchase.
