### 5. [Physicists trap a single antimatter particle for a month](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/physicists-trap-a-single-antimatter-particle-for-a-month/)
⭐ 63 💬 23 👤 mooreds
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50186450)

Physicists have trapped a single positron, an antimatter particle, for a month using electromagnetic fields in an ultracold vacuum, allowing direct study of its quantum states. The team plans to measure the positron’s magnetic moment with extreme precision to test whether it exactly mirrors the electron’s, as predicted by the Standard Model. Any difference could help explain why the universe contains matter rather than antimatter.

### 7. [How close can pure functional programming get to C?](https://rtfeldman.com/fp-vs-c)
⭐ 132 💬 19 👤 stmonty
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50218195)

Pure functional programming can approach C-level performance in compression workloads, as a Lean 4 DEFLATE library outperformed a widely used Rust implementation but still trailed a highly optimized C library. A Roc-based DEFLATE package, after extensive optimization, ran only a few percent slower than the C library on non-streaming benchmarks. The results suggest functional languages can get close to C performance, though hand-tuned C with architecture-specific optimizations remains ahead.

### 10. [How to run a city builder on 16 MHz](https://larstofus.com/2025/11/16/how-to-run-a-city-builder-on-12-5-mhz/)
⭐ 23 💬 6 👤 rex64
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50194536)

The article explains how the original SimCity could run a full city simulation on a 16 MHz 386SX by spreading its workload across frames. Its open-source code shows that the main Simulate function cycles through a 16-step counter, updating only selected systems such as traffic, fire, power, and crime each frame. This frame-slicing approach greatly reduced CPU load while keeping gameplay responsive enough for a city builder.
