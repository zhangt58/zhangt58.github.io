## Today's Top 10 on Hacker News

### 1. [Investigation of Polymarket Insider Trading on the Reinstatement of Jensen Huang](https://deucalionn.vercel.app/polymarket-delit-initie-jensen-huang-nvidia/)
⭐ 104 💬 43 👤 oska
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50626578)

An on-chain investigation alleges that a disposable Polymarket wallet bought large positions in a market on Jensen Huang rejoining NVIDIA shortly before public statements by Donald Trump and an NVIDIA announcement. The trade reportedly moved from a low probability to resolution, producing a substantial profit and suggesting access to nonpublic information. The case is presented as evidence that decentralized prediction markets can expose insider trading that may be hidden in traditional markets.

### 2. [2035 Came Early](https://fzakaria.com/2026/11/23/2035-came-early)
⭐ 348 💬 117 👤 setheron
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50624497)

The article describes how trynix.dev now runs Nix packages, including GUI applications, directly in the browser by using unkernel, a WebAssembly-based Linux engine. This makes it possible to execute more than 300,000 Nix package versions with much faster performance than earlier QEMU-based emulation. The author frames this as an early arrival of the long-predicted future where web technologies can host virtually any software.

### 5. [Stalwart is now a drop-in replacement for Microsoft Exchange](https://stalw.art/blog/microsoft-exchange-replacement/)
⭐ 512 💬 271 👤 shaunpud
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50620349)

Stalwart 1.2.0 is now a drop-in replacement for Microsoft Exchange, implementing MAPI over HTTP, Exchange Web Services, ActiveSync, and Autodiscover so existing Outlook and mobile clients can connect with mail, calendars, contacts, tasks, and notes. The open source release targets organizations seeking to leave on-premises Exchange as Microsoft shifts support toward cloud services and restricts on-premises functionality. It offers a self-hosted alternative that preserves data sovereignty while maintaining familiar client compatibility.
