### 5. [Show HN: I've updated my Linux System Call website and API](https://syscall.sh/)
⭐ 32 💬 14 👤 guitmz
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50334704)

The article announces an updated website and API that collects Linux system call tables for 27 architectures, including syscall numbers, arguments, registers, and history from Linux 3.3 through 7.3. It serves as a centralized reference for developers working with Linux system calls across different CPU architectures.

### 6. [F/OSS Comics #13: BSD Unix, Part 3 – The Beginning of BSD Unix](https://fosscomics.com/posts/13.%20BSD%20Unix%20Part%203%20-%20The%20Beginning%20of%20BSD%20Unix/)
⭐ 145 💬 15 👤 joonehur
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50332163)

After the first Berkeley Software Distribution, improvements and new tools such as vi and the C shell were packaged into 2BSD and distributed on tape. The arrival of a VAX at Berkeley highlighted the need for virtual memory, especially for symbolic computation work, and helped shape BSD Unix’s development. Meanwhile, ARPANET and DARPA interest in portable Unix made Berkeley’s distributions influential across research institutions.
