### 8. [Partial Operator Precedence](https://ziglang.org/devlog/2026/#2026-10-24)
⭐ 60 💬 31 👤 tosh
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340195)

Zig has implemented partial operator precedence, requiring parentheses when combinations of operators have ambiguous evaluation order. Common expressions such as arithmetic and comparison chains can remain parenthesis-free, reducing the number of precedence rules programmers must memorize. Existing code can be updated manually or with zig fmt --upgrade, and the compiler will flag any remaining ambiguous cases.
