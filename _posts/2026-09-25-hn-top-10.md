### 4. [Pentium II at 600Mhz with Voodoo 3 Emulated on 86Box with M6 Mac Mini](https://nyaa.sh/reviews/mac-mini-m6-emulation)
⭐ 79 💬 28 👤 hugh4life
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49841285)

86Box emulates old PCs at the hardware level, but its workload is dominated by a single host thread, making single-core speed and sustained clock more important than core count. The M6 Mac Mini’s strong single-thread performance lets it run a 600MHz Pentium II with an emulated Voodoo 3 at a stable 100% emulation speed, while 650MHz produced audio underruns. Improved ARM support in 86Box 6.0, including a JIT recompiler for Voodoo graphics, also helps the setup perform well.

⭐ 15 💬 10 👤 harscoat
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49842707)
