### 1. [Switzerland. Block by block. At 1:1 scale](https://blocks.manifold3d.ch/)
⭐ 140 💬 53 👤 theanonymousone
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50240922)

A Minecraft project recreates Switzerland at a true 1:1 scale, with each block representing one metre and terrain, glaciers, lakes, forests, and dams built from swisstopo open geodata. The Matterhorn and other landmarks are modeled to match real-world height and steepness. A free Jungfrau-Aletsch UNESCO area is available to test the setup, while the full country is offered as an early-access package.

### 3. [PaperPilot – Zero-Cloud, Pure-Rust PDF Engine with WASM and MCP](https://github.com/KrushnaVardhanReddy/PaperPilot)
⭐ 151 💬 44 👤 vardhan1237
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50238739)

PaperPilot is a PDF and document toolkit written in pure Rust that runs entirely locally — your files never leave your machine. A single engine powers an MCP server (letting Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any MCP client merge, split, OCR, and compress PDFs), a CLI, a browser-based WASM version, a Tauri desktop app, and a local REST API. Current features cover merging, splitting, rotating, compressing, pure-Rust OCR via PaddleOCR, watermarking, and PII scanning, with no Chromium or C++ runtime required.

### 7. [Growing Trees](https://brashandplucky.com/2026/10/15/growing-trees.html)
⭐ 74 💬 9 👤 ibobev
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50209278)

The article describes a procedural simulation for generating realistic tree shapes by modeling seasonal growth. Buds compete for light, with a growth resource allocated through the branching structure so well-lit buds produce new shoots while shaded branches are pruned. The method uses a voxel shadow grid or space colonization to estimate light and growth potential, producing emergent crown shapes.

### 8. [Show HN: A Truly Modular Keyboard](https://github.com/notaroomba/keybytes)
⭐ 173 💬 57 👤 NotARoomba
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50228115)

Keybytes is an open-source modular keyboard where every key is its own 1u PCB with a CH32V003 RISC-V microcontroller, connected via pogo pins on all four sides so keys can be freely rearranged into any layout. A stacked STM32WB55 main board provides Bluetooth, USB-C, 2MB QSPI flash for key firmware, battery charging, and an RGB status LED. The case holds modules together with magnets, and the full design is shared in KiCad and Onshape.

### 9. [Overgrown, when plants invade your abandoned tabs](https://charliegerard.dev/blog/overgrown-extension/)
⭐ 291 💬 100 👤 speckx
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50197108)

Overgrown is a Chrome extension that turns neglected browser tabs into overgrown digital jungles, with plants spreading from the edges as tabs sit unread. Once a tab is sufficiently covered, its content becomes inaccessible until the user drags a mouse to slash away the vines, using selectable tools like a machete, chainsaw, or flamethrower. The project is a playful creative experiment rather than a productivity tool, adding a fun obstacle to revisiting old tabs.

### 10. [Superhuman Hardware Design Is the Easy Part](https://www.a16z.news/p/superhuman-hardware-design-is-the)
⭐ 23 💬 8 👤 gmays
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50210181)

Frontier AI models are rapidly approaching superhuman ability to generate engineering-grade hardware designs, including CAD, PCBs, and part sourcing. The core difficulty is the lossy interface between digital designs and physical reality, which complicates manufacturing, measurement, and iteration. As a result, even models that can one-shot electromechanical designs will still need extensive work to produce reliable physical hardware.
