### 7. [Classic PC demoscene productions running natively in the browser](https://treylorswift.github.io/demoscene-recomp/web/)
⭐ 82 💬 60 👤 adunk
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50002426)

A project allows classic DOS demoscene productions to run directly in web browsers by recompiling their original code into WebAssembly. The process records the demos executed instructions, translates them with precise timing, and adds models for the timer, VGA graphics, and Sound Blaster audio. This lets the demos loaders, music, and visual effects behave as they originally did while only the hardware is simulated.

### 8. [How did Rosalind Franklin miss the helix in her iconic DNA image? She didn't](https://www.science.org/content/article/how-did-rosalind-franklin-miss-helix-her-iconic-dna-image-she-didn-t)
⭐ 184 💬 66 👤 pavel_lishin
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49969073)

A new analysis of Rosalind Franklin's laboratory notes shows she recognized the helical structure of DNA in an earlier x-ray image, photograph 49, and used it to calculate key measurements of the spiral. This finding challenges the long-standing claim that she failed to understand the significance of the famous photograph 51, which she later produced as a more refined version. The work restores Franklin's scientific insight while still acknowledging Watson and Crick's broader model of DNA.

### 9. ['Jonathan' is the oldest land animal on Earth](https://www.404media.co/oldest-living-land-animal-jonathan-the-tortoise/)
⭐ 168 💬 86 👤 gumby
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49998066)

Jonathan, an Aldabra giant tortoise and the world's oldest living land animal at about 194 years old, has had his genome sequenced to help explain his exceptional longevity. Researchers found unique gene variants in aging-related pathways, including DNA repair, insulin regulation, mitochondrial function, and unusually stable methylation patterns. The study offers early clues about the biological mechanisms behind extreme lifespan in tortoises.
