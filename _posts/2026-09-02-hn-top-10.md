### 3. [The Emergent Symbolic Structure of Artificial Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29530)
⭐ 174 💬 62 👤 schmuhblaster
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49531651)

A study shows that the internal vector representations of neural networks, including large language models, can be closely approximated by explicit symbolic structures. Replacing a network's representation process with a closed-form symbolic equation leaves its behavior largely unchanged in arithmetic, logic, code, and language tasks. This suggests modern AI implicitly realizes symbolic structure, offering a bridge between vector-based learning and traditional symbolic intelligence.

### 4. [LLM Judges Verify Presence, Not Absence: Omission Blindness in AI Clinical Notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31016)
⭐ 12 💬 2 👤 sbulaev
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49534583)

A study shows that LLM judges used to audit AI-generated clinical notes are good at detecting added or altered information but poor at detecting omitted facts. Standard single-note checks fail, while restructuring the task to list transcript facts and verify each one recovers reliable omission detection. The work provides a benchmark and methods for improving safety checks of ambient clinical scribes.

### 6. [Why are there no flow batteries with symmetric ferrocyanide electrolytes?](https://chemisting.com/2026/09/02/why-are-there-no-flow-batteries-with-symmetric-ferrocyanide-electrolytes/)
⭐ 7 💬 0 👤 DamonHD
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49534396)

Ferrocyanide/ferricyanide flow batteries are usually asymmetric because ferrocyanide is unstable when cycled far from its redox potential. At low anode potentials it decomposes to iron and free cyanide, while high cathode potentials also destroy it, and it forms insoluble Prussian blue with many metal cations. These issues make a common-electrolyte symmetric design impractical for useful battery voltages.

### 10. [Introducing Ad Blocker for Firefox on iOS](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/ad-blocker-on-ios/)
⭐ 510 💬 168 👤 HieronymusBosch
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49521973)

Firefox for iOS now includes an optional built-in ad blocker that uses Apple's WebKit Content Blocker and EasyList to stop many third-party ads and trackers before they load. The feature gives iOS users more browsing control in a platform where traditional ad-blocking extensions are unavailable.
