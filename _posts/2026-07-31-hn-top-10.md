### 6. [Show HN: Gander, an Android file viewer that asks for no permissions at all](https://github.com/mokshablr/gander)
⭐ 82 💬 36 👤 mokshablr
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49119425)
