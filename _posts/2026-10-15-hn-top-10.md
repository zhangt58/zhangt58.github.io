### 7. [LFM2.5-24B-A2B: Built for fast on-device agents](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-24b-a2b)
⭐ 220 💬 51 👤 gmays

LFM2.5-24B-A2B is a 24-billion-parameter reasoning model with only 2 billion active parameters, designed for fast, private on-device agents. ### 8. [The First Social Network: In rural Argentina residents send messages w AM radio](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/10/19/the-first-social-network)
⭐ 29 💬 10 👤 bookofjoe

### 9. [Catch a Bird – I made it for my own sanity](https://catchabird.com)
⭐ 198 💬 53 👤 eduardsik
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50053384)

Catch a Bird is an anonymous online experiment inspired by the Hungarian saying that a very happy person could catch birds. Visitors post short sentences about what made them happy, which appear as birds on a shared tree that others can catch, feed to keep them longer, and eventually let go. The site is free and public, with no sign-up, and each person can keep a personal bird book of sentences that touched them.

### 10. [Americans' social activity has steeply declined in the past 30 years](https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2026/9/29/americans-social-activity-has-steeply-declined-in-the-past-30-years)
⭐ 417 💬 388 👤 hubraumhugo
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50089921)

A new survey of U.S. adults finds that participation in many traditional social activities has fallen sharply over recent decades, with double-digit declines in hosting guests, attending club meetings, going to worship services, picnics, card games, and volunteering. In contrast, some activities have grown, especially internet use, home video gaming, exercise classes, and attending concerts. The findings add to evidence that Americans are becoming less socially connected in everyday life.
