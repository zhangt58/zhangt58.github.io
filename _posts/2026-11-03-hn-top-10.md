## Today's Top 10 on Hacker News

### 1. [Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/)
⭐ 2749 💬 834 👤 dbreunig
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341174)

The article describes a technique called Weight-Space Programming, in which large language models directly inspect and modify neural network weights without gradient-based training. It reports that an LLM can infer a network's intended task from raw weights, generate small game-playing networks, and improve a Qwen model's math performance by making tiny weight edits. These edits reportedly boost math benchmark scores substantially and also improve instruction-following.

### 2. [Nvidia – open-source auto-ontology](https://github.com/NVIDIA/auto-ontology)
⭐ 104 💬 32 👤 campervans
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50348962)

NVIDIA Auto Ontology is an open-source library that builds a semantic context layer for AI agents from enterprise metadata. It generates and reconciles ontologies across systems, grounds metrics and entities with trusted business meaning, validates generated queries against certified definitions, and exposes the context to any agent harness via MCP, CLI, and API. It is Apache-2.0 licensed, uses NVIDIA NIM models for inference, and currently does not accept external contributions.

### 3. [Toit v2.0.0](https://toit.io/blog/toit-v2-0-0/)
⭐ 117 💬 37 👤 floitsch
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50321605)

Toit v2.0.0 is the first major release of the open-source Toit language after years of alpha development, following the shutdown of its original company and continued community maintenance. The release finalizes a major breaking change that lets peripheral APIs accept pin numbers directly and adds broad hardware support, a unified command, a rewritten package manager, mixins, firmware envelopes, and compiler and runtime improvements. Future releases will continue adding features such as a formatter, compiler resolution improvements, and support for more architectures.

### 8. [European RISC-V Industrial Computing Initiative](https://erici.eu)
⭐ 136 💬 72 👤 ymz5
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50334523)

The European RISC-V Industrial Computing Initiative is developing an open, non-commercial reference stack for industrial computers based on RVA23 RISC-V hardware. It defines and validates the interfaces between firmware, boot software, a partitioning hypervisor, real-time control, and Linux, so that compatible systems can run isolated workloads on a single SoC. ERICI aims to close the gap between available RISC-V silicon and the reliability, field connectivity, and long-term independence needed for industrial use.

### 10. [Vathographs: Photographic depth maps as creative expression](https://vathography.com/)
⭐ 28 💬 6 👤 hankbond
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50331800)

Vathography is a creative practice that transforms ordinary photographs into depth-based artworks by using AI to infer the relative distance of every point in a scene. Instead of relying on light and color, it treats volume as the primary element, allowing artists to remap focus and emphasize different distances through tone and contrast. This turns a normally hidden technical depth map into a new way of seeing and composing photographic subjects.
