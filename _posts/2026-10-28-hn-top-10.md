### 1. [Wealth Inequality in America drawn to scale](https://dreamagainusa.org/)
⭐ 82 💬 37 👤 haraball
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50270764)

The article visualizes U.S. household wealth by percentile, showing that the median household holds about $211,000 while the 99th percentile holds over $15 million and the average top-1% household holds about $40 million. It emphasizes how extreme the top of the distribution is, with the average top-0.1% household holding nearly $184 million and the top-0.01% household holding about $971 million. Along the way, it highlights how low wages, inflation, and benefit eligibility cliffs can trap lower-income households.

### 3. [Git for the US Code](https://lexifina.com/blog/git-for-the-us-code)
⭐ 283 💬 139 👤 alansaber
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50268380)

The article proposes treating the United States Code like a version-controlled codebase, where every statutory provision can be traced to the public law that created or changed it, the date the change took effect, and any retroactive scope. It argues this is especially important because current legal research increasingly relies on language models that can misapply superseded or temporally invalid rules. The author suggests that a checkable, machine-readable history of statutory changes would make legal interpretation more reliable and could be verified by reconstructing code versions from enacted laws.

### 9. [Tailscale SSH: Investigation and remediation update](https://tailscale.com/ssh-investigation-update)
⭐ 293 💬 154 👤 figmert
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50264857)

Tailscale is investigating security vulnerabilities in Tailscale SSH that could allow an authorized user to perform actions beyond their configured permissions, including potential privilege escalation. The company is preparing a patched client release and recommends users review SSH access policies, disable SSH forwarding if unnecessary, and tighten access to sensitive systems until the update is available. It does not currently recommend disabling Tailscale SSH entirely and will publish further details once fixes are complete.
