### 1. [The group chat where your AI talks behind your back](https://construct22.com/)

### 3. [Fields-winning mathematicians worried about eerie silence over AI and encryption](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/technology-uk/article/ai-making-online-security-useless-smr73d3n8)
⭐ 45 💬 37 👤 conradqh

The concern is that AI may make current encryption obsolete before safeguards are in place.

### 4. [YouTube Ad Punisher – Auto-reloads YouTube pages when an ad plays](https://github.com/antonpictures/youtube-punisher)
⭐ 429 💬 488 👤 iantongeorge
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50657530)

A browser extension that auto-reloads YouTube pages the moment an ad appears, preserving playback position so the video resumes exactly where it left off. It sidesteps YouTube's anti-adblock detection and the wait for the skip button — the author notes two reloads are typically enough to clear an ad.

### 8. [CTO of 'RansomwareHelp' indicted for wire fraud](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/cto-of-ransomwarehelp-indicted-for-wire-fraud/)
⭐ 280 💬 139 👤 Bender
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50657645)

The CTO and co-founder of RansomwareHelp was indicted for wire fraud after an FBI sting revealed the company did not use its claimed proprietary decryption tools. Instead, it secretly paid ransomware attackers for decryption keys and then charged victims a markup for services it said it never performed. The company had publicly told victims not to pay ransoms and claimed high success rates in recovering encrypted data.

### 9. [Company Culture = Slack Emoji ÷ √Headcount](https://lia.mg/posts/measuring-company-culture-with-slack-emoji/)
⭐ 400 💬 187 👤 liamg
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50659640)

The article humorously argues that company culture is better measured by Slack custom emoji than by surveys, using a formula that divides emoji count by the square root of headcount. It also suggests tracking the percentage of employees who upload emoji to gauge whether culture is broadly shared or dependent on a few individuals. The piece uses satirical score bands to describe organizational health from deadpan to chaotic.

### 10. [European Commission Declares Browser Competition a Digital Markets Act Priority](https://open-web-advocacy.org/es/blog/commission-responds-declares-browser-competition-a-dma-priority/)
⭐ 71 💬 40 👤 anilgulecha
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50664191)

The European Commission has declared browser competition a priority under the Digital Markets Act, recognizing browsers as a key entry point to the web. It said it is working to ensure rival browsers can effectively compete with Safari on iPhones and iPads. The statement followed an open letter backed by a broad coalition of digital rights groups, publishers, consumer advocates, and technology companies.
