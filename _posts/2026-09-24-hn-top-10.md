⭐ 28 💬 0 👤 Luc
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49802661)

### 10. [Contrastive Language Models](https://contrastive-lm.notion.site/)
⭐ 82 💬 17 👤 erichocean
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49826221)

Contrastive Language Models are a new class of fast decision-making models trained with a contrastive objective that links states to actions. The released CLM-8B matches Jev on computer-use, gaming, and tool-calling tasks while running up to nine times faster, and with lightweight fine-tuning it achieves state-of-the-art results on agentic coding benchmarks. The work also introduces efficient training and serving infrastructure and establishes scaling laws for contrastive language models.
