### 3. [The Mythical Man-Month in the Age of AI](https://j11g.com/the-mythical-man-month-in-the-age-of-ai)
⭐ 171 💬 122 👤 ilreb
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50255298)

The article examines whether Brooks’s Law still applies when AI agents replace human developers. It argues that while agents can remove some coordination overhead, sequential work and the need for clear requirements, review, and orchestration remain. As a result, adding agents to a poorly specified project may simply produce incorrect results more quickly.

### 7. [New 'Circadian' Subtype of Depression Affecting 1 in 4 Patients](https://www.sciencealert.com/researchers-identify-new-circadian-subtype-of-depression-affecting-1-in-4-patients)
⭐ 63 💬 32 👤 gmays
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50196212)

Researchers identified a circadian subtype of depression in about one quarter of patients, characterized by persistent jet-lag-like symptoms such as delayed sleep, social jet lag, evening chronotype, and morning fatigue. The study suggests depression is not a single disorder and that recognizing this subtype could help match patients to more effective treatments. It also found the subtype often began earlier and involved more severe features, including weight gain and higher risk of mania, psychosis, or suicidality.

### 8. [Sebastian Raschka's from-scratch video course on building reasoning models](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/building-reasoning-models)
⭐ 719 💬 99 👤 sgiannopoulos
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50245138)

The course also clarifies how reasoning models function as the core engine inside AI agent frameworks that add context management and tool use.
