### 1. [Meta Ignores Brazilian Court Order to End Bolsonaro Favoritism](https://www.panamericandispatch.org/p/meta-ignores-brazilian-court-order)

[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50229587)

### 3. [Cerebras Deploys Trillion-Parameter AI Models](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/how-we-deploy-trillion-parameter-frontier-models-on-cerebras)
⭐ 185 💬 62 👤 gmays
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50227356)

Cerebras explains how to run trillion-parameter frontier models on its WSE-3T wafer-scale chips by partitioning model layers across multiple wafers. Using the 3-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 model as an example, it shows that 4-bit quantized layers fit on 44 GB wafers, with dense and linear-attention layers mapped differently to accommodate KV caches. The resulting 59-wafer pipeline passes only activations between chips over RDMA, enabling multi-trillion parameter inference at 1000 to over 2000 tokens per second.

⭐ 50 💬 18 👤 bookofjoe
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50193936)

A workshop in Nishi-Izu, Japan preserves a traditional hand-fired smoke-drying method for making katsuobushi, Japanese dried bonito. Fresh bonito is filleted, boiled, repeatedly smoked over a wood fire, repaired with bonito paste, fermented with koji, and sun-dried for months until it becomes as hard as wood and rich in umami.

### 6. [The Goose Programming Language](https://aardappel.github.io/goose/)
⭐ 423 💬 258 👤 bobbydigitales
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50224100)

Goose is a memory-safe systems programming language that avoids heap allocation, garbage collection, and lifetime annotations by keeping dynamic values on compiler-managed data stacks. It claims performance and memory advantages over C++ and Rust, with features such as inline nested data, position-independent relative references, in-place construction, and isolated threads. The language aims to provide safe, high-performance systems code with familiar C and Rust syntax and no unsafe or manual memory management.

### 7. [Rewriting a Commercial Engine in Jai](https://polygonjs.com/blog/why-i-rewrote-polygon-from-scratch)
⭐ 27 💬 15 👤 frading

A developer is rewriting their commercial procedural-geometry engine, Polygonjs, from JavaScript and TypeScript to the new language Jai. ### 8. [Cannabis legalisation and cardiovascular hospitalisations in the USA](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/16/10/e111050)
⭐ 8 💬 6 👤 gumby
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50202964)

A nationwide US analysis examined whether legal recreational cannabis sales were associated with cardiovascular hospitalisations. It found that each year of legalisation was linked to higher rates of hospitalisation for atrial fibrillation or flutter, heart failure, and myocardial infarction, while no significant increases were seen for ischaemic stroke, venous thromboembolism, or ventricular tachycardia. The authors conclude that cannabis legalisation may lead to meaningful increases in cardiovascular healthcare use.
