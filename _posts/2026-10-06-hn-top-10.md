### 2. [Beam: Reflection's 501B open-weight model](https://reflection.ai/blog/introducing-beam)
⭐ 468 💬 148 👤 Philpax
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49969183)

Beam is Reflection's first open-weight model, a sparse mixture-of-experts architecture with 501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active parameters, designed for coding, reasoning, and agentic workloads. It was trained on 23.8 trillion tokens and reinforced with large-scale RL, producing performance competitive with larger open models while using substantially less inference compute. The model is positioned as an efficient enterprise option for coding and agentic tasks.

### 5. [Dust: Pretraining Transformers Without Backpropagation](https://qlabs.sh/research/dust)
⭐ 206 💬 50 👤 E-Reverance
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49970871)

Dust is a zeroth-order training method for transformer language models that replaces backpropagation by perturbing activations independently at each token and using a single forward pass to estimate gradients. It is competitive with backprop at large compute budgets and far more efficient than weight-space evolutionary search, with larger models showing better population efficiency. The method’s gradient estimates remain well aligned with backprop across scales up to 1B tokens, suggesting it could scale to larger pretraining.

### 7. [Opus 5.5 agents discover two room-temperature magnetic semiconductor candidates](https://www.vals.ai/blogs/room-temperature-magnetic-semiconductors)
⭐ 366 💬 245 👤 outlier99
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49970667)

A team of Opus 5.5 AI agents and a researcher identified two room-temperature magnetic semiconductor candidates with properties useful for spintronic memory. The work focuses on Luttinger-compensated antiferromagnets, which combine the fast switching and dense packing of antiferromagnets with the spin sorting needed for spintronics. One candidate was newly designed, while the other was an existing material first made in 1999 whose predicted properties match the target.

### 9. [Resurrecting iChat Audio and Video Conferencing](https://blog.pipetogrep.org/2026/09/11/resurrecting-ichat-audio-and-video-conferencing/)
⭐ 56 💬 13 👤 thepipetogrep
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49973878)

The article explains how to revive iChat audio and video calls on old Macs running Leopard or Show Leopard by adding a hosts file entry that points configuration.apple.com to a working server. This lets iChat discover its public IP through a SNATMAP server and establish peer-to-peer RTP connections, provided the router preserves outbound NAT source ports. The author also recounts the history of iChat AV and the debugging steps used to identify why modern HTTPS redirects broke the old client.

### 10. [Example.com just launched the biggest redesign in decades](https://www.debugbear.com/blog/example-dot-com-redesign-history)
⭐ 233 💬 152 👤 jgx0
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49971921)

Example.com, the reserved domain used for documentation examples, has undergone a major redesign that adds JavaScript-driven multilingual support, cycling through several languages with animated text transitions. The changes also include a book icon and a split page structure intended to reduce bandwidth, since most traffic is automated and does not load the JavaScript. IANA emphasizes the site is a placeholder for illustrative use, not a service for testing or monitoring.
