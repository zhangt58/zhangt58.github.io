### 8. [Greg Kurtzer's Ginux is now a userspace kernel](https://github.com/gmkurtzer/ginux)
⭐ 83 💬 40 👤 d3Xt3r
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50450007)

After finishing an experiment as a standalone Rust kernel that booted via UEFI and ran a Rocky Linux userspace, Ginux pivoted to a configurable userspace kernel that runs as an ordinary Linux process. It provides isolated Linux compatibility instances under an explicit, versioned contract, deciding per syscall whether to emulate, mediate, or deny it — deliberately breaking the full Linux ABI guarantee in a controlled, testable way rather than competing with Linux itself.
