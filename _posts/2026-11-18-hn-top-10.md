### 3. [Multiple vulnerabilities in Regexp and String case conversion](https://www.ruby-lang.org/en/news/2026/11/18/multiple-vulnerabilities-in-regexp-and-string/)
⭐ 68 💬 14 👤 runesoerensen
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50554038)

Seven vulnerabilities were found in Ruby's regular expression engine and string case conversion, including heap buffer overflows, double frees, integer overflows, and out-of-bounds reads. Some flaws can be exploited for remote code execution when crafted strings or regular expressions are processed. The issues are fixed in Ruby 4.0.10, 3.4.13, and 3.3.14, and upgrading is strongly recommended.
