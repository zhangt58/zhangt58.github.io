### 3. [Why don't more developers “use the platform”?](https://nolanlawson.com/2026/10/03/why-dont-more-developers-use-the-platform/)
⭐ 156 💬 124 👤 vinhnx
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49950554)

Developers often avoid native browser APIs because for much of the web's history, browsers were inconsistent and incomplete, making custom libraries a safer choice. Familiarity with package ecosystems and frameworks, along with better documentation and polished examples for third-party tools, also make npm solutions feel easier than raw platform features. As a result, many developers reach for ready-made libraries even when the browser could handle the task natively.

### 4. [So you think you could be an electrician?](https://asteriskmag.com/issues/15/so-you-think-you-could-be-an-electrician)
⭐ 252 💬 173 👤 zdw
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49910462)

The article challenges the popular claim that skilled trades are a better path than college, arguing that the narrative is often promoted by people with degrees and oversimplifies the realities of trade work. It notes that college graduates still tend to earn more over their careers, actual college costs are often lower than advertised, and a degree remains a reliable route to higher earnings. The piece suggests the choice between trades and college is more complicated than the AI-proof six-figure job story implies.

### 5. [The work by Valve's Timur Kristóf on improving old AMD GPUs on Linux](https://www.phoronix.com/news/XDC-2026-Valve-Timur-AMDGPU)
⭐ 307 💬 46 👤 speckx
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49946895)

Valve’s Linux graphics driver team has made significant improvements to the AMDGPU kernel driver for older AMD GCN 1.0 and 1.1 graphics cards. The work helps move these legacy cards from the older Radeon driver to the modern AMDGPU driver, enabling Vulkan support, better performance, and improved display and power management. These changes make decade-old AMD GPUs more practical for Linux gaming and other tasks.
