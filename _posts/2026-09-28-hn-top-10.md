### 1. [Parley: Federated, decentralised chat that speaks plain IRC](https://git.mills.io/prologic/parley)
⭐ 9 💬 4 👤 davidcollantes
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49875913)

Parley is a federated, decentralized chat network that lets each person or team run their own domain instance and communicate over plain IRC. Instances discover one another through DNS and signed HTTPS messages, allowing users with email-style identities to send direct messages and join replicated channels using standard IRC clients. The project is currently a working proof of concept demonstrating the design end to end.
