### 10. [Claude reveals ECDSA curve origins](https://www.anthropic.com/research/ecdsa-curve-origins)
⭐ 884 💬 328 👤 ahlCVA
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50122800)

Claude, an Anthropic research model, solved a long-standing mystery about the origins of the NIST ECDSA elliptic curve seeds. It found that the seeds were not generated by hashing a phrase, but by sampling bytes from the C library's lrand48 random-number generator initialized with a Unix timestamp. The explanation settles years of speculation and increases confidence that the curves were not intentionally weakened.
