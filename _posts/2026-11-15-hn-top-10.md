### 2. [Why did some Apollo missions carry a grenade launcher?](https://space.stackexchange.com/questions/37775)
⭐ 63 💬 24 👤 Skyfall106
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50505271)

The grenades and launcher were part of an active seismic imaging experiment, not a security measure. By deploying a seismometer and detonating surface charges of known size, the astronauts could study how shockwaves propagated through the lunar subsurface, which let them infer the depth and structure of the Moon's internal layers.

### 4. [Court denies motion to dismiss DRAM price fixing case](https://litnews.ai/articles/garciaguirre-samsung-sk-hynix-micron-dram-conspiracy-suit-survives-dismissal)
⭐ 215 💬 75 👤 ybathaee
[🔗 Discuss on HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50502988)

A federal judge denied Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron’s motion to dismiss a proposed class action alleging they conspired to restrict conventional DRAM supply and shift capacity to high-bandwidth memory. The court found the complaint plausibly alleged a Sherman Act violation, distinguishing it from an earlier dismissed DRAM case and relying on factors such as long-term HBM commitments, refusal of conventional DRAM contracts, production decisions, and the companies’ prior cartel history. The case will proceed to the answer stage.
